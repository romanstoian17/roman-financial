# Voice Performance Pattern

Reference clip:

`https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6ALtylhifIE`

Use this as performance inspiration only. Do not clone, copy, or imitate the speaker's exact voice identity.

## What Works

- The voice does not stay at one emotional level. It rises slightly on tension and relaxes after the point lands.
- Short claims get their own space. A line like "Not always." should feel intentionally placed, not rushed into the next sentence.
- Explanatory parts move a little faster than emotional hooks or conclusions.
- Important nouns and contrasts get emphasis: old rate, new payment, cheap money, toll booth, planned too late.
- The tone feels like a real person thinking with the viewer, not an announcer reading a perfect paragraph.

## Roman Financial Adaptation

Roman should sound:

- conversational, but still researched;
- lightly animated, but not theatrical;
- warm enough for immigrant-money stories;
- skeptical of easy answers, but not cynical;
- paced like a person explaining something important across a table.

## Performance Recipe

Use **micro-beat grouped generation** for final voiceovers. Split narration into smaller meaning beats for audio performance, then group those beats back into longer visual scenes so images do not flash too quickly.

Recipe:

1. Hook: medium speed, curious, short pause inside the question.
2. Turn: slower, short enough to land, but not so slow it drags.
3. Mechanism: faster and cleaner.
4. Contrast: medium-slow, with a pause before the implication.
5. Example/metaphor: medium speed, warmer.
6. Takeaway: slightly slower, grounded.

Default speed range:

- Hook opening: `0.96` to `1.00`
- Viewer assumption / contrast: `0.84` to `0.92`
- Main explanation: `0.98` to `1.07`
- Caveat: `0.90` to `0.97`
- Metaphor / closing: `0.90` to `0.96`

Default pause range:

- Inside hook: `80ms` to `360ms`
- After short turns: `450ms` to `600ms`
- Between explanation beats: `120ms` to `300ms`
- Before key contrast / takeaway: `300ms` to `450ms`

## Audio Effects Later

Do not start with heavy effects. First improve performance with pacing and segmentation.

Possible later polish:

- light compression for steadier loudness;
- gentle EQ to add presence;
- very subtle room tone under pauses;
- soft background music at low volume only after voice choice is stable.
