# Script Writer Storytelling Validation

This validation uses the same factual material from
`projects/from_zero_to_options_v1/voiceover.txt`: Roman's family arrived in
Canada from Ukraine in 2022, most available money went into an old car, they
were renting, house prices looked impossible, they decided to try anyway, and
the habit was investing 150 CAD per week.

## Before: Factual Chronological Style

In 2022, we came to Canada from Ukraine with two kids. Almost all the money we
had went into an old car. We were renting because we had left our apartment back
home. Then I looked at houses and saw prices around eight hundred thousand,
nine hundred thousand, and sometimes one million dollars. It felt impossible.
After that, my wife and I decided to try saving and investing anyway. We
started learning about brokers, accounts, credit, taxes, employer matching, and
cashback. We decided to invest one hundred and fifty dollars per week.

## After: Storytelling Style

The first big financial decision we made in Canada was not inspiring.

It had four wheels, it was old, and it took almost all the money we had.

That car was supposed to help us start a new life: work, school, groceries,
normal family things. Then I opened the housing listings and saw homes around
eight hundred thousand, nine hundred thousand, sometimes a million dollars.

For a moment, saving for a home did not feel difficult.

It felt like the idea had been deleted.

So my wife and I were left with a strange little question: if the goal looks
impossible, do you quit, or do you test whether the system has another door?

Our test was not dramatic. One hundred and fifty dollars a week. Less than a
day of income. Small enough to feel almost silly next to Canadian house prices,
but clear enough that we could repeat it while we learned the rules: brokers,
accounts, credit, taxes, employer matching, cashback, all the things nobody
hands you in a welcome package.

Same facts. Different job: the second version gives the viewer a moment, a
problem, a decision, and a reason to keep listening.
