## Main Source Argument, Paraphrased

The video frames the AI market boom as a financing story, not only a stock-price story. The central concern is that AI infrastructure requires massive borrowing and long-term commitments. If expected AI cash flows arrive late, the stress may show up through debt, private credit, insurers, and structured finance channels before investors fully understand the exposure.
