## Core Topic

The AI stock boom may be less fragile because people are excited, and more fragile because the infrastructure buildout is being financed with enormous fixed obligations: data centers, chips, power contracts, loans, bonds, and private credit.

## Added Context

- AI infrastructure requires huge capital spending before future profits are proven.
- Hyperscaler capex is expected to stay extremely high.
- Data-center financing can move risk away from public stock headlines and into debt/private-credit markets.
- Insurance companies and private-credit structures matter because they are large holders of long-duration credit.
- The simple investor question is: are AI cash flows growing fast enough to service the financing stack?

## Target Viewer
