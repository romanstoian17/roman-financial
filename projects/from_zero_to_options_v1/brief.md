# From Zero To Options

## Working Title

We Came to Canada With No Down Payment. Four Years Later, We Had Options.

## Channel Role

This is the first channel-defining story for Roman Financial: money after immigration, starting from zero, and learning the financial system one decision at a time.

## Promise

Show how small repeatable investing decisions, plus learning the local money system, can change a family's options over several years.

## Target Viewer

Immigrants, newcomers, and first-generation builders who feel behind financially after moving to a new country.

## Personal Facts

- Arrived in Canada from Ukraine in 2022.
- Family includes Roman, his wife, and two kids.
- Most available money went into an old car.
- Family rented after leaving the apartment in Ukraine.
- No serious investing experience before arriving.
- Started investing roughly `150 CAD` per week.
- Roman chose individual stocks for his accounts.
- His wife used ETF-style investing because she was not interested in stock picking.
- Current investment accounts are approximately `58,600 CAD` and `54,500 CAD`, about `113,100 CAD` combined.
- A nearby similar house recently appeared around `527,000 CAD`, meaning the family now has more than 20% of that price in invested capital.

## Tone

Personal, honest, practical, and not boastful. The lesson is not "copy my exact investments." The lesson is that small repeatable actions plus learning the system can create choices.

## Compliance Note

Educational content only. Not financial, investment, tax, legal, or mortgage advice. This is a personal story, not a recommendation to buy specific stocks, ETFs, or a house.
