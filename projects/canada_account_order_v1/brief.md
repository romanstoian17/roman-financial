# Canada Account Order

## Goal

Create a 60-second Roman Financial preview inspired by Canadian personal-finance explainer channels such as Canadian Finance with David, but using original wording, original visuals, and the recurring simple Roman guide character.

## Topic

Which Canadian account should come first: TFSA, FHSA, or RRSP?

## Why This Topic

It is beginner-friendly, evergreen, strongly Canadian, and useful for a short video. It also creates clear visual story scenes: three account doors, first-home path, employer match, flexible TFSA bucket, and a simple decision flow.

## Source Notes

Verify before publication with official/current sources:

- CRA TFSA annual dollar limit, eligibility, withdrawals, and restored contribution room: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/tax-free-savings-account.html
- CRA FHSA participation room, deductible contributions, and qualifying withdrawals: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/first-home-savings-account.html
- CRA RRSP deduction room rules and annual limit: https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/services/tax/individuals/topics/rrsps-related-plans.html
- Employer RRSP matching plan details vary by workplace.

## Tone

Friendly Canadian finance explainer. Educational, not personalized advice.

## Runtime

Aim for about 60 seconds.

## Disclaimer

Educational content only. Not financial, investment, tax, or legal advice.
