# Family Cast Pipeline Test

## Purpose

Test whether the Roman Financial family cast works inside the actual video pipeline: generated family scenes, Fish voiceover, smooth Ken Burns movement, and rolling-highlight subtitles.

## Scope

This is a short 45-60 second preview using the first story:

- Arrival in Canada with two kids and an old car.
- Housing prices feel impossible.
- Family decision to try anyway.
- Researching brokers, accounts, and beginner investing tools.

## Visual Direction

Use the approved colorful graphic-novel ink-wash finance explainer style. Use `library/characters/family_cast_v1/family_lineup_v1_reference.png` and the individual character references as anchors. Wife defaults back to `wife_v3_reference.png`.

## Success Criteria

- Family members remain recognizable enough across scenes.
- Ages stay stable, especially daughter 17 and son 8.
- Wife uses V3 clothing by default.
- Images feel integrated, not avatar overlays.
- Audio, subtitles, and scene changes stay synchronized.

## Promise

Explain the money decision with a simple, practical framework.

## Target Viewer

A beginner who wants a clear answer without hype.

## Notes
