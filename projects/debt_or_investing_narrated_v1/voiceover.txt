Most people think investing is always the grown-up move.

But if your money is sitting beside high-interest debt, the math changes fast.

A credit card charging twenty percent does not wait politely while your investments try to catch up.

That is why the first win is not always buying something new. Sometimes it is stopping the leak.

Keep a small emergency buffer, then attack the expensive debt with focus.

Once that pressure is gone, investing is no longer fighting yesterday's bill.

The smartest move is not the most exciting one. It is the one that gives your next dollar the most power.
