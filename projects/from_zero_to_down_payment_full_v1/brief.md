# From Zero To A Down Payment

## Working Title

We Came to Canada With No Down Payment. Four Years Later, We Had Options.

## Channel Role

This is the first full Roman Financial channel-defining story: money after immigration, starting with no down payment, learning the Canadian financial system, and building enough stability to have choices.

## Audience

Immigrants, newcomers, and first-generation families who feel financially behind after moving to a new country.

## Story Facts

- Roman and his family arrived in Canada from Ukraine in 2022.
- Family includes Roman, his wife, a 17-year-old daughter, and an 8-year-old son.
- Most available money went into an old car.
- The family was renting after leaving their apartment back in Ukraine.
- Roman had no serious investing background before moving.
- The family decided to invest about 150 CAD per week.
- Roman picked individual stocks in his accounts.
- His wife used a simpler ETF-style path.
- Current approximate account values: Roman 58,600 CAD, wife 54,500 CAD, combined 113,100 CAD.
- A similar nearby home was recently listed around 527,000 CAD, creating the possibility of more than 20% down payment.

## Tone

Personal, grounded, practical, and reflective. Not a flex. Not a guru story. The point is that small repeatable actions plus learning the system can create options.
