# From Zero To A Down Payment - Full Episode Outline

## Working Title

We Came to Canada With No Down Payment. Four Years Later, We Had Options.

## Core Promise

Show newcomers that the first real financial milestone is not becoming rich. It is becoming stable enough to have choices.

## Target Runtime

8-9 minutes for the first full generated draft.

## Structure

1. **Cold Open Hook**
   - We arrived in Canada in 2022 with two kids.
   - Most of our available money went into an old car.
   - House prices looked impossible.
   - Four years later, we had roughly enough invested capital for more than 20% down on a nearby similar home.

2. **Why This Is Not A Flex**
   - This is not a story about being rich.
   - It is a story about learning a system after immigration.
   - Educational, not personal financial advice.

3. **Starting Point**
   - Renting after leaving the apartment in Ukraine.
   - Two kids, car costs, insurance, groceries, normal bills.
   - No serious investing background.

4. **The Impossible House Moment**
   - Seeing prices around 800k, 900k, 1M.
   - Feeling that the possibility did not exist.
   - The emotional shift from panic to a practical question: what can we control?

5. **The Decision**
   - Roman and wife decide to try anyway.
   - No perfect plan.
   - Just a small weekly commitment.

6. **Learning The System**
   - Brokers.
   - Investment accounts.
   - Employer match.
   - Cashback.
   - Credit.
   - Taxes.
   - Future-video hooks.

7. **Beginner Investing**
   - Roman liked researching individual stocks.
   - Wife preferred a simpler ETF path.
   - Explain ETFs as baskets of companies.
   - No stock recommendations.

8. **The $150 Per Week Habit**
   - Small amount.
   - Less than a day of income.
   - Felt pointless at first.
   - Consistency mattered more than excitement.

9. **The Boring Middle**
   - No applause.
   - Numbers grow slowly.
   - Life still looks normal.
   - Same car, same rent, same bills.

10. **Results After Four Years**
    - Roman accounts around 58,600 CAD.
    - Wife accounts around 54,500 CAD.
    - Combined around 113,100 CAD.
    - Numbers are approximate and personal.

11. **The Shift From Dream To Option**
    - Similar nearby house listed around 527k CAD.
    - More than 20% down payment exists as a possibility.
    - The question changed from "can we ever?" to "should we?"

12. **Takeaways**
    - Start with the system, not the perfect investment.
    - Automate small decisions.
    - Use tools you understand.
    - Build capital to buy options.

13. **Channel Promise**
    - Money after immigration.
    - Mistakes, tools, accounts, broker choices, taxes, housing, cars, credit, and investing.
    - Journey from zero to options.
