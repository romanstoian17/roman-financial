# Pay Off Debt or Invest First?

## Promise

Help beginners understand the simple order of operations when they have both debt and an urge to start investing.

## Target Viewer

Someone with credit card debt, a small amount of savings, and uncertainty about whether investing now is responsible or premature.

## Core Lesson

High-interest debt can quietly beat most realistic investment returns. The practical order is: keep a small emergency buffer, attack high-interest debt, then invest consistently once the expensive leak is under control.

## Boundaries

This is educational content, not personalized financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Do not promise investment returns. Use example numbers only.
