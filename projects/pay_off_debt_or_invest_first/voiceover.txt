Should you pay off debt, or start investing first?

Here is the simple way to think about it.

If your credit card charges around twenty percent, and your investment might earn seven percent over time, the math is not close.

Investing while high-interest debt grows is like filling a bucket while water leaks out the bottom.

First, keep a small emergency buffer, so one surprise bill does not send you backward.

Then attack the expensive debt: credit cards, payday loans, anything with a painful interest rate.

After that, investing becomes much cleaner, because your money is no longer fighting against yesterday's bills.

The goal is not to be perfect. The goal is to put your next dollar where it helps you the most.

This is education, not personal financial advice.
