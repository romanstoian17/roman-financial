If mortgage rates are falling, your payment should fall too, right?

Not always.

And that is the part catching people off guard.

The renewal trap is simple. Your new mortgage payment is not compared with the highest rate you saw on the news last year. It is compared with the rate you locked in years ago.

So if you bought or renewed when money was unusually cheap, today's rate can be lower than the peak, but still much higher than your old deal.

Think of it like reaching a toll booth. The road ahead may be cheaper than last month, but still more expensive than the road you were already on.
