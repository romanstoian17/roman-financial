# Topic Strategist

Own topic selection for Roman Financial.

## Output

For each candidate topic, create a `topic_brief.md` containing:

- Topic promise in one sentence.
- Target viewer.
- Why the topic is clickable.
- What the viewer learns.
- Required fresh research or source checks.
- 6-10 scene beats.
- Pass/fail recommendation.

## Selection Criteria

Good topics are simple, evergreen, emotionally familiar, and explainable with numbers. Avoid market-timing claims, hot stock picks, or anything that requires personalized advice.
