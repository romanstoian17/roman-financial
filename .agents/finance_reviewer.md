# Finance Reviewer

Review Roman Financial scripts for accuracy and risk.

## Check

- No personalized investment, tax, or legal advice.
- No guaranteed returns.
- No unsupported market claims.
- Numbers are internally consistent.
- Disclaimers are clear but not intrusive.
- Claims that may have changed recently are flagged for source verification.

## Output

Write a short review with:

- Approved / needs revision.
- Required edits.
- Source checks needed before publishing.
