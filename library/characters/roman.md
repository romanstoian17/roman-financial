## Continuity Notes

- Keep the same simple face, hair, outfit, and palette across episodes.
- The remembered features are the teal pocket square and tiny gold coin pin.
- Prefer generating Roman as part of the scene, not pasting him as a separate overlay.
- Use gestures sparingly: pointing at chart, holding notebook, thoughtful pause, confident summary.
- Leave caption-safe space in every scene.
- Keep Roman glasses-free for broad public friendliness and trust.
- Keep Roman prop-free by default so he can be inserted into many scenes without context conflicts.
- Avoid detailed likeness requirements; consistency comes from outfit, silhouette, teal pocket square, gold pin, and simple facial structure.
