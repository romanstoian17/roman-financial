# Roman Character V1

## Role

Family-inspired Roman character for Roman Financial stories. Use as the recurring host, father, and narrator figure in immigrant-family finance scenes.

## Privacy Direction

Inspired by real photos, but not an exact portrait. Keep him simplified, public-safe, trustworthy, warm, and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Locked Visual Features

- Adult Ukrainian-Canadian father and finance storyteller.
- Short light-brown hair with a slightly textured top.
- Trimmed short beard and mustache.
- Blue or gray-blue thoughtful eyes.
- Fair complexion.
- Calm confident expression with a small friendly smile.
- Slim-to-average build.
- Signature clothing: dark burgundy overshirt or casual jacket over a white or black T-shirt, dark casual pants, clean sneakers.
- Signature detail: small green accent such as zipper pull, pen, or watch detail.

## Style

Colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, polished but approachable.

## Prompt Block

Family-inspired Roman character, adult Ukrainian-Canadian father and finance storyteller, short light-brown hair with slightly textured top, trimmed short beard and mustache, blue or gray-blue thoughtful eyes, fair complexion, calm confident expression with a small friendly smile, slim-to-average build, dark burgundy overshirt or casual jacket over a white or black T-shirt, dark casual pants, clean sneakers, small green accent such as zipper pull, pen, or watch detail, colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, trustworthy, warm, analytical, approachable, public-safe and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Avoid

- Do not make him photorealistic.
- Do not copy the reference photos exactly.
- Do not use sunglasses on his face unless the scene specifically needs it.
- Do not make him overly corporate, flashy, or influencer-like.
- Avoid logos, readable text, distorted hands, and exaggerated heroic proportions.
