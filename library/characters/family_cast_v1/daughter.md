# Daughter Character V1

## Role

Family-inspired 17-year-old daughter for Roman Financial stories. Use as a recurring supporting character in immigrant-family finance scenes.

## Privacy Direction

Inspired by real family photos, but not an exact portrait. Keep her simplified, public-safe, and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Locked Visual Features

- Teenage daughter, 17 years old.
- Shoulder-length wavy blonde hair.
- Large thoughtful eyes and natural brows.
- Soft rounded facial features.
- Friendly, calm, observant expression.
- Signature clothing: pale mint or pale blue simple top, jeans, white sneakers.
- Signature detail: small blue-green hair clip.

## Style

Colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, polished but not overly cute.

## Prompt Block

Family-inspired 17-year-old daughter character, shoulder-length wavy blonde hair, large thoughtful eyes, natural brows, soft rounded facial features, friendly calm observant expression, pale mint or pale blue simple top, jeans, white sneakers, small blue-green hair clip, modest casual clothing, colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, public-safe and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Avoid

- Do not make her photorealistic.
- Do not copy the reference photos exactly.
- Do not sexualize.
- Do not use party/birthday styling unless the scene specifically needs it.
- Avoid logos, readable text, distorted hands, and overly childish proportions.
