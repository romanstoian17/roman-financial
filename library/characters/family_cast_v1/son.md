# Son Character V1

## Role

Family-inspired 8-year-old son for Roman Financial stories. Use as a recurring supporting character in immigrant-family finance scenes.

## Privacy Direction

Inspired by real family photos, but not an exact portrait. Keep him simplified, public-safe, child-safe, and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Locked Visual Features

- Boy, 8 years old.
- Short light-blond hair with a slightly messy straight fringe.
- Bright curious eyes.
- Round youthful face.
- Friendly open smile.
- Energetic but sweet expression.
- Signature clothing: bright green or teal hoodie, dark T-shirt, jeans, sneakers.
- Signature detail: bright green hoodie plus slightly messy blond fringe.

## Style

Colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, polished but not overly cute.

## Prompt Block

Family-inspired 8-year-old son character, short light-blond hair with slightly messy straight fringe, bright curious eyes, round youthful face, friendly open smile, energetic but sweet expression, bright green or teal hoodie, dark T-shirt, jeans, sneakers, child-safe modest clothing, colorful graphic novel ink-wash finance explainer style, expressive graphite ink lines, watercolor texture, warm cream paper, public-safe and easy to regenerate consistently.

## Avoid

- Do not make him photorealistic.
- Do not copy the reference photos exactly.
- Do not sexualize.
- Do not use costume, plastic-bag hood, or party styling unless the scene specifically needs it.
- Avoid logos, readable text, distorted hands, and overly baby-like proportions.
