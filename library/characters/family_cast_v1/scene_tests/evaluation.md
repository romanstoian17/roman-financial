## Result

The cast is usable inside full story scenes. Signature colors survived well:

- Roman: burgundy overshirt plus green accent.
- Wife: coral/rust top and dark lower outfit.
- Daughter: blonde hair, pale mint/blue top, blue-green hair clip.
- Son: bright green/teal hoodie and blond fringe.

## Issues To Control

- Daughter can drift slightly older or more polished in close table scenes.
- Wife can become more stylized/idealized than intended if prompts overemphasize appeal.
- Wide scenes preserve characters better than close, crowded table scenes.
- Hands and papers are acceptable for tests, but close-up budget scenes still need regeneration checks.

## Prompt Rules For Future Scenes

- Always include age locks: `17-year-old daughter` and `8-year-old son`.
- For the daughter, add: `teenage proportions, not adult, no glamour styling`.
- For the wife, add: `natural, modest, appealing but not glamorous`.
- Prefer medium-wide framing when all four family members appear.
- Avoid asking for too many visible hands unless the scene needs it.
- Keep in-image documents abstract with no readable text.

## Recommendation

Use the family cast in the first video, but generate images scene-by-scene with manual approval. Do not fully automate character scenes until the cast survives at least one short 45-60 second video test.
