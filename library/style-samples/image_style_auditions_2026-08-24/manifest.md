# Image Style Auditions - 2026-08-24

Goal: compare more colorful visual directions for Roman Financial while keeping subtitles readable and the style repeatable across generated scenes.

Sample scene:

A newcomer in Canada sitting at a kitchen table with a budget notebook, house keys, calculator, a simple house model, and a down-payment savings jar.

## Options

1. `01_current_pencil_gouache_baseline.png` - current restrained pencil/gouache baseline.
2. `02_vibrant_watercolor_editorial.png` - more colorful watercolor, still organic.
3. `03_bold_editorial_magazine.png` - bold, colorful, high YouTube readability.
4. `04_clean_flat_editorial.png` - clean, simple, consistent, beginner-friendly.
5. `05_colorful_3d_clay.png` - friendly 3D clay look, very readable but more playful.
6. `06_graphic_novel_ink_wash.png` - cinematic, colorful, stronger story energy.
7. `07_paper_cut_collage.png` - consistent, colorful paper-cut style.
8. `08_retro_modern_poster.png` - boldest adult poster direction, memorable.
9. `09_cinematic_digital_painting.png` - beautiful and mature, but consistency risk is higher.
10. `10_bright_semi_real_editorial.png` - polished semi-real look, but face/hand consistency risk is higher.
11. `11_premium_explainer_illustration.png` - clean, colorful, repeatable explainer style.
12. `12_adult_storybook_editorial.png` - warm, charming, colorful, mature.
