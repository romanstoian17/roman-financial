- Canadian-specific personal finance angles.

## Financial Historian Pattern Notes

Use Financial Historian as a close format reference for narrator-led, image-driven finance storytelling.

Observed recent topic patterns:

- Economic history connected to current money behavior.
- Bubbles, banking power, currency wars, bailouts, housing, wages, and market reality.
- Titles often frame a hidden mechanism or contradiction, for example: "The Economy Looks Strong. So Why Is Everyone Broke?"
- Shorts often use one compact historical lesson, for example bubbles, devaluation, banking laws, or powerful finance families.

Roman Financial should adapt the **structure**: strong thesis, short narrated beats, matching illustration per beat, slow camera movement, subtitles, and no persistent host overlay.
